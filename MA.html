2 Connect the ears — optional

Deepgram = accurate transcription with speaker labels (free $200 credit at deepgram.com). Blank = free browser engine.
To hear the other people on the call (even with earphones), keep the audio source on “Mic + call audio” when you press Start — Chrome will ask you to share a screen/tab; pick the call's browser tab and tick “Share tab audio.” Your mic alone can't capture remote participants.

🎧 Using Zoom / Teams desktop app? One-time audio setup (BlackHole)
Desktop apps don't expose their audio to the browser, so route it through the free BlackHole virtual device:
  1. Install BlackHole (run the included “Setup BlackHole.command” in this folder, or brew install blackhole-2ch).
  2. Open Audio MIDI Setup → ➕ → Create Aggregate Device; tick your microphone and BlackHole 2ch. Name it “Meeting Capture”.
  3. ➕ → Create Multi-Output Device; tick your earphones/speakers and BlackHole 2ch (so you still hear the call).
  4. In macOS Sound → Output, pick the Multi-Output Device during calls.
  5. Here, set audio source to “My mic only” and choose “Meeting Capture” in the 🎧 input dropdown. That single device now carries your voice and the call.
Then you can use earphones freely — BlackHole taps the audio before it reaches them.
